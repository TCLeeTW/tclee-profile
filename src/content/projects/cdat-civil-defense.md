why:
  heading: "Built from zero, because the neighborhood layer didn't exist."
  paragraphs:
    - "Taiwan's civil defense conversation mostly happens at the national policy level or in the abstract (\"whole-of-society defense\"). The neighborhood layer — the people who'd actually help each other in the first hours after a major event, before institutions can respond at scale — didn't have an organization behind it in Neihu/Ganghu. I founded CDAT (Civil Defense Advancement Taiwan) and its community training unit, 港湖民防團, to build that layer from zero."
how:
  heading: "No template, no shortcuts."
  items:
    - "Started with no institutional backing and no template to copy."
    - "Built a structured curriculum across multiple tracks: TECC/BLS emergency medical care, radio and mesh communications (including TAK/ATAK), drone operations, and home hardening/evacuation planning."
    - "Ran weekly training sessions plus quarterly field exercises to keep the group active rather than a one-off class."
